Why the ideal estimate overshoots: the paper's limits imply ~3.1e7 heralded rounds, which is what a Werner-equivalent visibility of about 0.90 needs (n_min = 2.5e7 at V = 0.90), whereas eta_l = 0.97 alone gives V_eff = 0.94 (n_min = 4e6). So the paper's photonic model carries additional noise (T = 0.01 events that are not fully suppressed, possibly also a smaller P_H prefactor) that its text does not specify.

Also found: Eq. (D1) prints sqrt(t/(1-t)) but the cited source (Gonzalez-Ruiz et al., Eq. 1) and a beam-splitter derivation give t/(1-t). They agree at t = 1/2, the only value used, so no result is affected.

## 5. What counts as an independent check

The choice between the two protocol readings (sifting factor in the EC cost, `ec_model`) was made by comparing against Fig. 5 and Fig. 6, so **those matches are calibrations, not blind predictions**: they show the paper's numbers are consistent with these readings, not that its printed equations produce them (the literal Eq. B11 + B1 combination gives V_min = 0.91 at n = 1e10). Checks that were not tuned: the Fig. 4 trade-off, the Fig. 3 slope and ordering, completeness, message-length insensitivity, the Delta-L scaling, the 370 h / 1248 h arithmetic, n_s, and the internal consistency of the two ideal-source distances.
